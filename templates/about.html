About The System

A comprehensive educational platform designed to empower students to evaluate, understand, and enhance their campus recruitment readiness through machine learning and multi-dimensional assessments.

Project Overview

The Student Placement Prediction System is an intelligent machine learning portal developed to give students transparent, data-driven feedback on their campus recruitment chances.

Traditional placement preparation is often fragmented, with students lacking visibility into how their GPA, backlogs, technical certifications, communication skills, and quantitative aptitude interact during campus drives.

This system bridges that gap by combining a trained Machine Learning classification model (80% weighting) with rule-based multi-factor assessments (20% weighting) to output dependable readiness scores and actionable study recommendations.

Why This System?

Campus placements demand diverse competencies. A high GPA alone cannot guarantee success without sharp communication, practical problem-solving logic, and targeted preparation for specific company recruitment patterns. Our system provides structured assessments and diagnostic analysis to guide every student toward career success.

Hybrid Prediction Methodology

80% Machine Learning Model

Predicts placement probability based on empirical student performance features: CGPA, previous semester performance, verified internships, extracurricular participation, project count, and calibrated communication score.

20% Rule-Based Assessment Profile

Comprises Technical Skills (45 pts), Quantitative Aptitude (30 pts), Verified Certifications (up to 15 pts), and Communication Test (10 pts) to reflect comprehensive candidate quality.
